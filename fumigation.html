Fumigation Service

We provide comprehensive Fumigation and Pest Control Services designed to protect your home or business from unwanted pests. Our expert team uses advanced methods and safe, effective treatments to eliminate infestations and prevent future occurrences. We offer three options for fumigation based on the method of dispersion to cater to different needs and preferences:

Regular Spray Fumigation:

This method involves using a standard motorized chemical spray to target and eliminate pests. It is highly effective for a wide range of pests and provides quick results. However, it may have a noticeable odor during application.

Odourless Spray Fumigation:

For clients sensitive to strong smells or those who require a more discreet solution, we offer odourless spray fumigation. This method uses specially formulated chemicals that are equally effective but do not leave behind any lingering odors, ensuring minimal disruption to your environment.

Fogging (Gas):

This method involves dispersing a gaseous pesticide using a fogging machine, allowing the gas to penetrate hard-to-reach areas and crevices. Fogging is ideal for large spaces and severe infestations, providing thorough coverage and long-lasting protection.

Deratification

In addition to our fumigation services, we specialize in deratification, the process of eliminating rodent infestations. Our deratification services include:

Inspection and Assessment:

Conducting a thorough inspection to identify rodent entry points, nesting areas, and the extent of the infestation.

Trapping and Removal:

Using humane traps and removal methods to capture and eliminate rodents from your property.

Rodenticide Application:

Applying safe and effective rodenticides in targeted areas to eradicate rodent populations.

Exclusion Techniques:

Implementing preventive measures such as sealing entry points, installing barriers, and advising on best practices to prevent future infestations.

Monitoring and Maintenance:

Providing ongoing monitoring and maintenance services to ensure long-term rodent control and prevent recurrence.
